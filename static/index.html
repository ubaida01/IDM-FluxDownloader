GET THE MOST OUT OF FLUX

How Flux works

A real download manager, running on your own computer.

01

Grab any direct file

Paste an HTTP(S) link to a file. Flux transfers it directly and attempts to resume partial downloads when the server supports byte ranges.

02

Choose the best video quality

On supported sites, yt-dlp detects available streams. Best available downloads the source's top video and audio tracks; FFmpeg merges them when needed. Protected/DRM content isn't supported.

03

Control your queue

Pause, resume, cancel, retry, prioritize, and export your downloads. Queue history is stored in a local SQLite database.
